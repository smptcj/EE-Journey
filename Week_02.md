## Concepts Studied

* Digital Signals
* Breadboard Fundamentals
* Arduino Architecture

## Key Findings

* Analog signals can take any value within a continuous range.
* Digital signals operate using discrete states, typically HIGH and LOW.
* Breadboards enable rapid circuit prototyping without soldering.
* Arduino boards are built around microcontrollers that execute uploaded programs.
* Arduino programs continuously execute through the `loop()` function after initialization in `setup()`.

## Research

* Investigated common Arduino starter kit components.
* Learned how breadboard power rails and terminal strips are connected.
* Explored the structure of Arduino sketches and the role of input/output pins.

## Open Questions
